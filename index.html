Functions of the Skeletal System

Support

The skeleton provides shape and support to the body.

Protection

Bones protect vital organs like the brain, heart and lungs.

Movement

Bones and muscles work together to create movement.

Types of Bones

Long Bones

Femur, Tibia, Humerus.

Short Bones

Carpals and Tarsals.

Flat Bones

Skull, Sternum and Ribs.

Irregular Bones

Vertebrae and Sacrum.

Bone Diseases

Osteoporosis

Reduced bone density and fragile bones.

Scoliosis

Abnormal curvature of the spine.

Arthritis

Inflammation of joints affecting movement.
